About this reconstruction & sources

The Herodian Second Temple, around 30 CE. Layout and principal spaces follow ancient descriptions; detailed decoration, landscape, colors, paving placement and people are interpretive. Dimensions use an illustrative 0.5 m cubit. The Holy of Holies is empty.

Architectural references: Josephus, Jewish War 5 · Antiquities 15 · Mishnah Middot · Israel Museum model photograph · Excavated geometric stone flooring. Captured daylight: Poly Haven, Dry Meadow (CC0). Project on GitHub.
