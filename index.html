About this reconstruction & sources

The Herodian Second Temple, around 30 CE. Layout and principal spaces follow ancient descriptions. The sanctuary’s four-pilaster façade, deep gilded porch, dressed stone, botanical carving, gold-sheet work and worn steps form a crafted architectural interpretation; detailed profiles and finishes include imagined elements. Landscape, colors, paving placement and people are also interpretive. Dimensions use an illustrative 0.5 m cubit. The Holy of Holies is empty. Antonia’s courtyard, southern stair details and passage continuation are interpretive; the southern vestibules extend 45 metres into a schematic passage network.

Architectural references: Josephus, Jewish War 5 · Antiquities 15 · Mishnah Middot · Israel Museum model photograph · Excavated geometric stone flooring. Carved forms: Herodian smooth-leaf capital study · Triple Gate excavations · Southern approaches excavation report. Local material references: Marble 01 by Rob Tuytel · Dry Meadow daylight, from Poly Haven (CC0). These modern references do not reproduce an excavated Temple surface. Project on GitHub.
