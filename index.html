About this reconstruction & sources

The Herodian Second Temple, around 30 CE. Layout and principal spaces follow ancient descriptions; detailed decoration, landscape, colors, paving placement and people are interpretive. Dimensions use an illustrative 0.5 m cubit. The Holy of Holies is empty. Antonia’s courtyard, carved profiles, southern stair details and passage continuation are interpretive; the southern vestibules extend 45 metres into a schematic passage network.

Architectural references: Josephus, Jewish War 5 · Antiquities 15 · Mishnah Middot · Israel Museum model photograph · Excavated geometric stone flooring. Carved forms: Herodian smooth-leaf capital study · Triple Gate excavations · Southern approaches excavation report. Captured daylight: Poly Haven, Dry Meadow (CC0). Project on GitHub.
